# Benefits Guide

## Health Insurance

The company covers 90% of health insurance premiums for employees and 75% for
dependents. Enrollment opens within 30 days of the start date; changes outside
the annual window require a qualifying life event.

## Learning Budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 USD for courses, books,
and conferences. Amounts are per calendar year, do not carry over, and require
manager pre-approval for anything above 500 USD.

## Sabbatical

After five years of continuous service, employees may apply for an unpaid
sabbatical of up to six months. Applications go through the direct manager and
HR, and must be submitted at least 90 days in advance.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave; secondary
caregivers receive 6 weeks. Leave must start within 12 months of birth or
adoption.
