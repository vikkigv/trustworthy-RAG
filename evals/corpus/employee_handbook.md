# Employee Handbook

## Vacation Policy

Employees receive 24 vacation days per calendar year. Up to five (5) unused
vacation days may be carried over into the next calendar year; carried-over
days expire on March 31. Vacation requests must be submitted at least 14 days
in advance through the HR portal and approved by the direct manager.

## Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is
required for absences longer than three consecutive working days. Unused sick
days do not carry over.

## Remote Work

The company operates a hybrid model: a minimum of two days per week in the
office. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 in the employee's local
time zone. Employees working fully remotely from another country for more than
30 days must register with HR for tax purposes.

## Working Hours and Overtime

Standard working hours are 40 hours per week, Monday to Friday. Overtime must
be pre-approved and is compensated at 1.5x for weekdays and 2.0x for weekends
and public holidays.
