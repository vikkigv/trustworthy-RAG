# Security and IT Policy

## Passwords and MFA

All company accounts require passwords of at least 16 characters and
multi-factor authentication. Password rotation is required every 180 days.
Hardware security keys are mandatory for engineers with production access.

## Device Security

Company laptops must run disk encryption and the endpoint agent at all times.
Personal devices may access email only through the managed mobile app.
Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## Data Classification

Customer data is classified as Restricted and may never be copied to personal
storage or unapproved cloud services. Internal documents are Internal; press
releases and marketing material are Public. Sharing Restricted data externally
requires written approval from the CISO.

## Incident Reporting

Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@company.example
immediately, and never discussed on public channels. The on-call security
engineer responds within 15 minutes during business hours.
