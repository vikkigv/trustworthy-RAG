# Employee Handbook (2026 revision)

## Vacation Policy

Employees receive 24 vacation days per calendar year. Unused vacation days
do not carry over; all days expire at the end of the calendar year. Vacation
requests must be submitted at least 14 days in advance through the HR portal.

## Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 15 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is
required after two consecutive working days. Unused sick days do not carry
over.

## Remote Work

The company is now fully remote-first; the office remains optional. Employees
working from another country for more than 30 days must register with HR for
tax purposes.

## Working Hours and Overtime

Standard working hours are 40 hours per week, Monday to Friday. Overtime must
be pre-approved and is compensated at 1.5x for weekdays and 2.0x for weekends
and public holidays.
